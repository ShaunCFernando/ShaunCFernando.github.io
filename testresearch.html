    Summer 2025: Comparing the Effects of Ergosterol vs Cholesterol on Antifungal Peptides - Pennsylvania Governor’s School for the Sciences - Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, PA
    The Pennsylvania Governor's School for the Sciences (PGSS) is a rigorous, highly selective, tuition-free summer enrichment opportunity focused on STEM disciplines. For the 2025 summer program, I was one of 72 students selected from an exceptional applicant pool of 603 students from across Pennsylvania. The program functions as an accelerated college semester, featuring courses in biology, organic chemistry, concepts of modern physics, discrete mathematics, and artificial intelligence and machine learning, taught by professors from Carnegie Mellon University and the Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center. The curriculum includes an independent research project, which will be published in Fall 2025.

    Summer 2023: Applying Data-Based Deep Transfer Learning to Enhance Infectious Disease Case Detection Hillman Academy Senior Research Intern - University of Pittsburgh, Pittsburgh, PA
    The Hillman Academy, hosted by the UPMC Hillman Cancer Center and University of Pittsburgh, is a competitive summer research program that provides high school students with hands-on experience in biomedical and cancer research. For the 2023-2024 year, I was one of 80 students selected from an exceptional applicant pool of over 500 students from across the nation. Through mentorship by leading scientists and clinicians, participants engage in independent research projects, attend professional development workshops, and present their findings at a formal symposium. The program emphasizes critical thinking, scientific inquiry, and exposure to careers in medicine and biomedical research.
